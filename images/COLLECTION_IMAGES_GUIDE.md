## 💡 Tips for Best Results

### Image Selection
- **Image 1**: Front view or main hero shot
- **Image 2**: Back view, side view, or detail shot

### Image Specs
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Recommended Size**: 600px × 900px (portrait orientation)
- **Minimum Size**: 400px × 600px
- **File Size**: Keep under 500KB for fast loading

### Image Quality
- ✅ High resolution
- ✅ Good lighting
- ✅ Clean background
- ✅ Professional composition

---

## 🔄 How It Works on the Website

1. **Default View**: First image (e.g., `summer-rain-1.jpg`) displays
2. **On Hover**: Left ‹ and Right › arrow buttons appear
3. **Navigation**: 
   - Click ‹ to go to previous image
   - Click › to go to next image
   - Loops seamlessly between the 2 images

## ✨ Want to Add More Images?

You can add more than 3 images per dress! Just:

1. Add more image files (e.g., `summer-rain-4.jpg`, `summer-rain-5.jpg`)
2. Update `collection.html` to include additional `<img>` tags
3. The carousel will automatically work with any number of images
