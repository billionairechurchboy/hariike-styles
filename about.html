Morayo OshodiCreative Director
Creative director

It began with her grandmother’s sewing machine.

Morayo Oshodi is a designer whose journey began with a childhood fascination for craftsmanship. What started as quiet moments watching stitches come to life soon grew into a lifelong passion for fashion design.

Although she pursued a BSc in Physiology and an MSc in Environmental Toxicology and Pollution Management, her creative passion never waned. To broaden her perspective, Morayo studied Fashion Design and Marketing at IFA Paris, an experience that shaped her approach to design and her understanding of the global fashion industry.

In 2018, Morayo founded Hariike Styles, bringing her vision of individuality and timeless elegance to life. Today, her designs embody a balance of structure, artistry and storytelling: pieces that are not just worn, but experienced.

“For Morayo, fashion is a language, and Hariike Styles is her voice.”
