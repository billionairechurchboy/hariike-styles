Welcome to Hariike Styles

Hariike Styles began not just as a brand, but as a journey-one stitched with resilience and a deep rooted desire to create something meaningful.
We officially started in 2018, and what began as a humble pursuit has since grown into a thriving fashion brand committed to craftsmanship and intentionality.
Every piece is thoughtfully made to reflect the elegance and confidence of the modern woman.
Over the years, Hariike Styles has evolved from a single sewing machine and tailor into a full creative house with skilled designers, expert beaders, and administrative leads working together to bring every vision to life.
We are a brand built from the ground up, and every design we create carries a piece of our journey-a story of growth, grace, and how far we have come.

Creative Director Story

Morayo Oshodi, Creative Director of Hariike Styles, is a designer whose journey began with her grandmother's sewing machine and a childhood fascination for craftsmanship. What started as quiet moments watching stitches come to life soon grew into a lifelong passion for fashion design.
Although she pursued a BSc in Physiology and MSc in Environmental Toxicology and pollution management, her creative passion never waned. To broaden her perspective, Morayo studied Fashion Design and Marketing at IFA Paris — an experience that shaped her approach to design and her understanding of the global fashion industry.
In 2018, Morayo founded Hariike Styles, bringing her vision of individuality and timeless elegance to life.
Today, her designs embody a balance of structure, artistry, and storytelling; pieces that are not just worn, but experienced.
For Morayo, fashion is a language, and Hariike Styles is her voice
